Autoclave Alert & Sterilization Protocol

Ensure all lab apparatus and culture media are correctly wrapped, plugged, or sealed before loading. Follow strict 121°C at 15 psi parameters.

Sterilization Timer

15:00 Min : Sec STERILIZING...

Apparatus Checklist

  • Glass Petri Plates Washed, dried, and securely wrapped.
  • Test Tubes & Durham Tubes Plugged with cotton; caps slightly loosened.
  • Conical Flasks & Beakers Necks plugged and covered with foil or kraft paper.
  • Warning Do not autoclave heat-sensitive plastics.

Wrapping Techniques

Petri Plates Protocol

Stack 5 to 10 clean glass petri plates. Wrap them tightly in heavy-duty kraft paper using the envelope fold. Secure with autoclave indicator tape.
